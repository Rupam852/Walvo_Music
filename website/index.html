Got Questions? We Have Answers

Is Walvo Music completely free to use?

Yes! Walvo Music is 100% free and open-source under the MIT License. There are no subscriptions, hidden fees, or advertisements.

Do I need to log in with a Google account?

No, login is optional! You can stream music, search, and download tracks without logging in. Logging in allows you to access your personal YouTube Music playlists and liked songs.

Is my personal data safe?

Absolute 100% privacy. There is zero telemetry, no tracking SDKs, no analytics, and no remote data collection. All your history and playlists stay strictly on your device.

How do I update the app in the future?

Walvo Music has a built-in Auto-Updater that checks for official releases directly from our GitHub repository.
